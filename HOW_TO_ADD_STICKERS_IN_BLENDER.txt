================================================================================
         HOW TO ADD STICKERS TO YOUR 3D AVATAR IN BLENDER (STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================

Why 2D image slapping failed previously:
----------------------------------------
3D scans and photogrammetry models break the face into hundreds of tiny,
distorted "UV islands". Pasting a 2D square image onto the flat texture causes
warping, cut-off edges, and seams across the cheeks and forehead.
Blender fixes this by projecting the sticker directly in 3D viewport space onto
the actual geometry, calculating all seams automatically.


================================================================================
METHOD 1: STENCIL BRUSH (RECOMMENDED - TAKES 2 MINUTES, BAKED INTO SKIN)
================================================================================
This is the cleanest method. It paints the sticker directly onto the existing
avatar skin texture with zero extra meshes or performance loss.

STEP 1: IMPORT MODEL
--------------------
1. Open Blender.
2. Delete the default Cube and Lamp (select them and press X -> Delete).
3. Go to: File -> Import -> glTF 2.0 (.glb/.gltf)
4. Choose: "base_basic_pbr.glb" (located in your models/ folder).

STEP 2: GO TO TEXTURE PAINT WORKSPACE
------------------------------------
1. Click the "Texture Paint" tab at the very top of Blender.
2. In the 3D Viewport on the right, align your camera straight at the face
   by pressing Numpad 1 (or View -> Viewpoint -> Front).

STEP 3: LOAD YOUR STICKER AS A STENCIL TEXTURE
----------------------------------------------
1. Look at the right-side properties panel. Select the "Active Tool" tab
   (the screwdriver & wrench icon at the top of the properties column).
2. Scroll down to the "Texture" subsection and click "+ New".
3. Now go to the "Texture Properties" tab (the red checkered icon at the very
   bottom of the properties column).
4. Set "Type" to "Image or Movie".
5. Under "Image", click "Open" and select your transparent PNG sticker file.
6. Return to the "Active Tool" tab (screwdriver icon).
7. Under "Texture Mapping", change the dropdown from "View Plane" to "Stencil".
8. (Optional) Check "Image Aspect" so your sticker doesn't stretch.

STEP 4: POSITION & STAMP THE STENCIL
------------------------------------
A semi-transparent preview of your sticker will appear over the 3D viewport.
Use these mouse shortcuts while hovering in the 3D viewport:
  - Right-Click + Drag: Move the sticker position
  - Shift + Right-Click + Drag: Scale / resize the sticker
  - Ctrl + Right-Click + Drag: Rotate the sticker

Once positioned over the cheek, forehead, or nose:
- Left-Click and brush over the sticker to stamp it onto the avatar's face!
- Rotate the viewport slightly to inspect the result from different angles.

STEP 5: CRITICAL STEP - SAVE THE TEXTURE BEFORE EXPORTING
---------------------------------------------------------
Blender does NOT automatically save painted textures into memory!
1. Look at the "Image Editor" panel on the left side of the screen.
2. In its top menu bar, click: Image -> Save (or Alt + S).

STEP 6: EXPORT THE FINAL GLB
----------------------------
1. Go to: File -> Export -> glTF 2.0 (.glb/.gltf)
2. In the export settings panel on the right:
   - Format: "glTF Binary (.glb)"
   - Under "Include": Check "Selected Objects" (make sure your avatar is selected)
   - Under "Data" -> "Mesh": Ensure "Normals" and "UVs" are checked
3. Save the file as: "gautham_pbr.glb" and overwrite the file in:
   models/gautham_pbr.glb and public/base_basic_pbr.glb.


================================================================================
METHOD 2: SHRINKWRAP MESH DECAL (FOR ULTRA-CRISP HIGH-RES STICKERS)
================================================================================
Use this if you want the sticker to remain razor-sharp without depending on the
avatar's diffuse texture resolution.

1. In Object Mode, press Shift + A -> Mesh -> Plane.
2. Move and scale the plane in front of the cheek/face area (G to move, S to scale,
   R to rotate).
3. Tab into Edit Mode, Right-Click the plane -> Subdivide (Number of Cuts = 3).
   Tab back to Object Mode.
4. Go to the Material tab (red ball icon) -> New Material.
   - Set Base Color to "Image Texture" and open your sticker PNG.
   - Connect the Alpha output of the Image Texture to the Alpha input of Principled BSDF.
   - Under Material Settings (bottom of panel), change "Blend Mode" to "Alpha Blend".
5. Add Modifier (wrench icon):
   - Choose "Shrinkwrap".
   - Target: Click the eyedropper and select the Avatar mesh.
   - Wrap Method: "Project" or "Target Normal Project".
   - Offset: Set to 0.001 m (this prevents flickering / z-fighting).
6. Select both the sticker plane and the avatar (Shift + Left Click both),
   press Ctrl + J to join them into a single mesh.
7. File -> Export -> glTF 2.0 (.glb) -> save as "gautham_pbr.glb".
